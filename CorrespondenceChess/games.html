A Memorable Game

Arild Haugen – Colin McNab

One of the most memorable games of my correspondence chess career was played against Scottish Grandmaster Colin McNab in the 1999 ICCF European Championship. The game developed into a highly complicated struggle with attacking chances for both sides and has continued to attract interest and analysis over the years.
