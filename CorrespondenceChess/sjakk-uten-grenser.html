About the Book

Chess Without Borders documents the history of Norwegian correspondence chess and the Norwegian Correspondence Chess Federation during its first seventy-five years.

Written by Reidar Gramstad, Thor Geir Harestad and Arild Haugen, the book combines historical articles, player portraits and 123 memorable games.

The book follows the development of correspondence chess from the era of postcards and letters through the age of computers and modern online servers.

The Authors

Reidar Gramstad

Former president of the Norwegian Correspondence Chess Federation and one of the leading historians of Norwegian correspondence chess.

Thor Geir Harestad

Author, journalist and chess historian with numerous publications on chess and local history.

Arild Haugen

Correspondence chess player, researcher and co-author of Chess Without Borders.
