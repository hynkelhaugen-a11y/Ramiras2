The Story Behind the Game

Among the many correspondence chess games played over several decades, Wild Horses remains one of the most memorable. The game combined strategic planning, tactical complications, and the type of deep analysis that makes correspondence chess unique.

The struggle attracted attention beyond the tournament itself and later became known among correspondence chess enthusiasts.

"Wild Horses" was later featured by chess historian Tim Harding in his writings on outstanding correspondence chess games.
