Tournament Career

Tournaments with Achieved GM-Norms

  • 1st GM-norm in EU VI - Preliminary (including the famous game against McNab!)
  • 2nd GM-norm with a 1 point margin in WC25 Semi-final.
  • 3rd GM-norm secured after winning the John Jordan Memorial. (games)
  • 4th GM-norm secured in OL15 Prelim games) after a long and tough struggle against Tim Murray. View Cross Table
  • 5th GM-norm secured in the EU-VI Team Final (games)

Throughout several decades of correspondence chess I have participated in national and international tournaments under the auspices of the ICCF and the Norwegian Correspondence Chess Federation.

These events include Olympiads, World Championship cycles, European Championships and memorial tournaments.

Major International Events

Olympiads

ICCF Olympiad participation including winning Final in Olympiad XV and representing Norway in others.

🏆 World Championships

Participation in ICCF World Championship cycles, making it all the way to the final.

🌍 European Championships

European team and individual events against many of the strongest correspondence players on the continent.

🎯 Other Tournaments

Memorial tournaments, Nordic championships and other international events contributing to ICCF Grandmaster norms.
