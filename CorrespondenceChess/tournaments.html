Tournament Career

Throughout several decades of correspondence chess I have participated in national and international tournaments under the auspices of the ICCF and the Norwegian Correspondence Chess Federation.

These events include Olympiads, World Championship cycles, European championships and memorial tournaments.

Major International Events

CC Olympiad XV Final

Member of the Norwegian team in the Olympiad Final against many of the world's strongest correspondence chess nations.

CC Olympiad XVII Final

Another Olympiad Final appearance representing Norway in international correspondence chess competition.

World Championship Semi-Final

Participation in the ICCF World Championship cycle and one of the strongest events of my correspondence chess career.

European Team Final

Competing at the highest European team level against leading international players.

Special Tournaments

  • John Jordan Memorial Tournament

  • European Preliminary Events
