About Ramiras2

Welcome to Ramiras2, a personal website devoted to correspondence chess, music, family history and the legacy of Ramiras.

Correspondence Chess

Correspondence chess, known in Norway as fjernsjakk, is chess played over distance. Historically moves were exchanged by post, often resulting in games lasting months or even years. Today correspondence chess is usually played on dedicated online servers, while maintaining the same emphasis on deep analysis and careful planning.

The game has produced a rich international tradition and has attracted many strong players, analysts and authors. Norway has a proud correspondence chess history through the Norwegian Correspondence Chess Federation (Norges Fjernsjakkforbund), founded in 1945.

This website contains selected games, historical material, tournament experiences and articles related to correspondence chess collected over many years.

About Arild Haugen

My interest in chess began in the 1950s when my father, the Norwegian magician Ramiras, introduced me to the game in Stavanger. What started as childhood curiosity soon developed into a lifelong passion for chess, analysis and competition.

After competing in local and regional events, I became deeply involved in correspondence chess. The attraction was the possibility of conducting detailed analysis and exploring positions at a depth rarely possible in over-the-board play.

Through many decades I have participated in Norwegian and international correspondence chess tournaments, won national titles and represented Norway in ICCF competitions. Correspondence chess has allowed me to meet players from around the world and to contribute to the rich tradition of analytical chess.

Together with Reidar Gramstad and Thor Geir Harestad, I co-authored Chess Without Borders – 75 Years of the Norwegian Correspondence Chess Federation 1945–2020. The book documents the development of Norwegian correspondence chess and includes 123 memorable games.

This website serves as a permanent archive of selected games, articles, photographs, chess history and personal memories accumulated through a lifetime in chess.
