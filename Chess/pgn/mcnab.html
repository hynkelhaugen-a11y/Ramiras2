A Memorable Game

Arild Haugen – Colin McNab

This correspondence game from the 1999 ICCF European Championship became one of the most memorable encounters of my chess career. My opponent was the Scottish Grandmaster Colin McNab, renowned for his opening expertise and deep theoretical knowledge.

The game developed into a fierce battle in which both kings came under sustained pressure. White sacrificed material for initiative and attacking chances, while Black sought active counterplay in the center. Analysis conducted over the years has shown that both players missed improvements along the way, making the struggle even more fascinating from a modern perspective.

“An extraordinarily complicated game”
– ChessBase commentary

After a long and highly tactical fight, White succeeded in maintaining the pressure, and Black eventually resigned in a position where decisive material loss could no longer be avoided.
