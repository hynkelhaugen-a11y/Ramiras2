About Music

Music has accompanied every stage of life. Some recordings are connected with particular memories, journeys and friendships, while others simply continue to reveal new qualities after many years of listening.

Just as correspondence chess rewards patience and exploration, music often reveals its deepest treasures over time. The pages in this section represent a personal journey through a lifetime of musical discovery.
