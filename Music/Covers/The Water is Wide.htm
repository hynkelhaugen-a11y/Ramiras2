The Water is Wide.    (VieVann)

 

Original :      Eriksen , men det var Reidar Larsen som lagte Sweet Home Chicago!

 

Best Cover:    Strengemusikken     Worst Cover:    Eric Clapton/Bob Dylan


Background: Some claim the song is Scottish based on a song from America - O, Waly, Waly. (words and sound)  Others say it's Irish written in 1724 based on the same song as above which is part of a longer ballad called Lord Jamie Douglas.    
Eva Cassidy has a beautiful version on her latest album American Tune!

There are several versions of the lyrics. (another one)  Irish version.                       Interesting story of two oaks - with music.

 Pat Conroy has written a book with the same title!  Interesting text!?
