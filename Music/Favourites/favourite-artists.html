Favourite Artists

The soundtrack of a lifetime

Armar & Bein

One of Rogaland's most original bands. Armar & Bein combines local humour, Stavanger dialect and memorable storytelling with roots in folk, rock and blues. The band began by performing songs by Tom Waits, Bob Dylan and Van Morrison translated into Stavanger dialect, before creating an extensive catalogue of original material.

Favourite Albums

  • Kardang
  • Lette Formiddagsstoner

Cowboy Junkies

The Canadian band Cowboy Junkies created a unique blend of folk, country, blues and alternative rock. Their breakthrough album The Trinity Session remains one of the most atmospheric recordings of the late twentieth century.

Favourite Albums

  • The Trinity Sessions
  • Black Eyed Man
  • Pale Sun Crescent Moon

Van Morrison

One of the most influential singer-songwriters to emerge from Belfast. Van Morrison's music spans folk, jazz, soul, blues and Celtic traditions. Albums such as Astral Weeks and Moondance are considered classics.

Favourite Albums

  • Astral Weeks
  • Moondance
  • Into The Music
  • Common One

Son Volt

Led by Jay Farrar, Son Volt became one of the defining groups of the Americana movement. Their mixture of country, rock and folk has influenced countless artists since the release of their landmark debut album Trace.

Favourite Albums

  • Trace
  • Straightaways
  • Wide Swing Tremolo

Helldorado

Helldorado blends Americana, desert rock, spaghetti-western atmosphere and Norwegian songwriting traditions. The Stavanger band has developed a loyal following both in Norway and internationally.

Favourite Albums

  • Lost Highway
  • Director's Cut
  • Bones In The Closet

Flying Shoes

Flying Shoes emerged from the Helldorado environment and focused on classic country rock and Americana. The project pays tribute to the rich traditions of American roots music.

Favourite Albums

  • Flying Shoes

Eriksen

Brothers Kurt and Frank Eriksen have been among Norway's finest songwriters for decades. Their music combines folk, country and melodic rock with thoughtful lyrics and strong vocal harmonies.

Favourite Albums

  • Two Blue
  • Alt vende tebage
  • Small Hall Classics

Dag Vagle

Best known as songwriter, guitarist and frontman of Helldorado, Dag Vagle has also established himself as a compelling solo artist. His songs blend cinematic storytelling, Americana influences and distinctive Norwegian perspectives.

Favourite Albums

  • Western Wave
  • Hello Radio
